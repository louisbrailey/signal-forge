Options — Education & Simulation

Phase 3. The options module is education and simulation only. It will cover: plain-language calls/puts, a break-even calculator, long-option maximum-loss, theta / time-decay visualisation, implied-volatility and earnings-event risk, and covered-call / cash-secured-put / defined-risk vertical simulators — all paper-only.
