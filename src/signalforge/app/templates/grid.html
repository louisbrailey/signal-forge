Mechanical Grid

{{ page_intro('A mechanical grid that buys a little on dips and sells a little on rises around an anchor price. Simulated only, and not judged by the strategy promotion gates.') }}
{{ chart_card('grid-act','Grid fills per day','/api/charts/grid?days=365', ranges=False, height=220, hint='Simulated dollars bought and sold each day.') }}

A mechanical rebalancing rule on daily closes: per symbol, buy a fixed dollar amount after a fixed percentage drop from the anchor, sell a fixed dollar amount after a fixed percentage rise, and move the anchor to the triggering close. Fills at the next session's open on a dedicated paper account.

The grid is not a promotion-gated strategy. It has no walk-forward evidence, no sealed test, and no approval scope — it never produces a candidate and never sends an alert. Every fill below is SIMULATED. Research signal only — no order was placed.
