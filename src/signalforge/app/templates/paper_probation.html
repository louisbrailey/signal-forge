Paper Probation

Supervised paper-trading evidence for every paper_approved strategy, restricted to its exact approved data snapshot. A candidate is alert-eligible only when its scan's dataset snapshot, universe, data source, and live strategy parameters all exactly match a passing promotion evaluation — anything else is blocked with NO TRADE — approval does not apply to this dataset.

Paper-approved does not mean profitable, and does not mean suitable for live trading. It means a strategy cleared the sealed walk-forward test on one specific historical dataset. Every trade here is simulated — research signal only, no order was ever placed.
