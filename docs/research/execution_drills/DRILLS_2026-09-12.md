## What a PASS here does and does not mean

These drills run against `brokers/paper_reference.py`: local, deterministic, network-free, and with fills the implementer controls. D6 §3.1 rule 4 fences those fills off from every gate, and nothing in this report is evidence about a strategy. A PASS means the **refusals fire** — that the kill switch blocks opening and not closing, that a divergence halts instead of repairing, that a timeout cannot double-fill. §21 requires the same list to be repeated against the vendor paper endpoint, with **zero** armed strategies, before any of it is evidence about a broker.
