# Disclaimer

**Signal Forge is a research, simulation, alerting, and education tool. It is not
financial advice. Execution features are disabled by default.**

- Every recommendation is a **hypothetical research output**, generated by
  deterministic, version-controlled rules. It is not a solicitation or a
  recommendation to buy or sell any security.
- Sandbox strategy execution requires explicit enablement and arming. Separate
  production broker operations require operator-provisioned credentials, exact
  account/environment binding and explicit confirmation of saved action terms.
  Neither research output nor AI text authorizes a trade or transfer. See
  `docs/decisions/0006-broker-access-expansion.md`. Manual CSV analytics stay offline.
  Brokerage websites are never automated; execution never stores credentials.
- Every alert and recommendation is labelled: **"Research signal only — no order
  was placed."**
- Market data may be **delayed, end-of-day, sandbox, or simulated**. The
  interface labels the latency and source of every value. Do not rely on it as
  real-time unless it is explicitly marked `REALTIME` with a confirmed
  entitlement.
- Backtested and paper-traded results are hypothetical, do not include all
  real-world costs, and **do not predict future results**. Out-of-sample and
  walk-forward results are shown where available and are the only basis on which
  any confidence figure is displayed.
- Options content is **education and simulation only**. Long options can expire
  worthless; multi-leg strategies carry additional risk.
- **You are responsible for independently verifying every input and for your own
  decisions.** Consult a licensed financial professional before trading.

The AI explanation layer, where enabled, only rephrases deterministic outputs and
summarizes source documents. Any AI-written text is marked **"AI explanation, not
a trading instruction."**
